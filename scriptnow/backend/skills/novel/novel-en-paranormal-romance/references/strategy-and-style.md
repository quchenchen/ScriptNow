# Paranormal romance strategy

Define what the bond can sense, compel, conceal, cost, and never prove. Give pack, clan, court, or bloodline rules material consequences and dissenting factions. Verify consent under compulsion, lore continuity, political causality, and power cost.

## “Werewolf skin” projects

When the project intentionally uses a light werewolf wrapper around a contemporary emotional plot:

- establish only the supernatural rules that change the relationship conflict;
- let rejection, pack rank, scent, heat, shifting, or mate recognition create concrete leverage or risk;
- keep the emotional line primary, but require at least one causal payoff that could not occur in an ordinary contemporary romance;
- do not spend scarce short-form words on encyclopedic pack history;
- do not let “fated mate” replace consent, repair, or an earned choice.

## Emotional escalation

Open near the rejection, betrayal, replacement, or public loss. Escalate by changing what the protagonist knows and can do: private hurt → social consequence → evidence of concealed motive → costly choice → earned reversal. A later regret or pursuit arc must be demonstrated through action, lost leverage, and repair—not declarations alone.
