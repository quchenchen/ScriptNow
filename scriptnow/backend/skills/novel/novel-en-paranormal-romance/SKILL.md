# English Paranormal Romance

Make the supernatural bond a source of information, pressure, temptation, and consequence—not automatic consent or emotional truth.

## Workflow

1. Read the bond rules, factions, character boundaries, and adopted facts.
2. Read [strategy and quality](references/strategy-and-style.md).
3. Make romance and fantasy causally change each other.
4. Preserve language, POV, tense, and latest human revision.
5. Review consent, lore continuity, political causality, and power cost.

## Boundaries

- Combine with one platform Skill and the shared quality Skill.
- Avoid alpha shorthand and destined-love replacing relationship work.
