# Story structures

Use only the structure requested in the CreativeProfile. Map stages to chapter or scene functions, not rigid chapter counts. Short fiction may merge stages while preserving their causal and emotional work.

## Hero's Journey (`hero-journey`)

Track departure, initiation and return as changes in identity, belonging and value—not only external adventure. Preserve call, threshold, ordeal, transformation and return when compressing.

## Three Act (`three-act`)

Act I establishes desire and commitment; Act II deepens causality, relationships and the midpoint reframe; Act III forces the thematic choice and shows its aftermath.

## Five Act (`five-act`)

Introduction, rising pressure, irreversible climax, consequences, and resolution. Each act must change both external conditions and the viewpoint character's available choices.

## Save the Cat (`save-the-cat`)

Opening image, theme stated, setup, catalyst, debate, break into two, B story, promise of premise, midpoint, pressure closes in, all is lost, dark night, break into three, finale, final image. Each beat must alter what the protagonist can safely believe or do.

## Eight Sequence (`eight-sequence`)

Use eight promise–complication–turn units. In short fiction, combine adjacent sequences but retain distinct causal turns; in long fiction, align units with chapter clusters rather than fixed chapter counts.

## Harmon Circle (`harmon-circle`)

Comfort, need, unfamiliarity, adaptation, attainment, price, return and change. Emphasize interior reinterpretation alongside external state changes.

## Freytag Pyramid (`freytag`)

Exposition, rising action, climax, falling action and denouement. Reserve enough narrative space after climax for consequence, recognition and thematic closure.

## Custom (`custom`)

Require user-defined stage names, order, completion conditions, target proportions, mandatory turns and compression rules before planning chapters.

## Genre overlays

Mystery tracks truth, investigator belief, reader belief, evidence provenance and false explanation separately. Romance tracks attraction, vulnerability, compatibility evidence, rupture, earned choice and the future relationship contract. These overlays supplement rather than replace the selected narrative structure.
