# English Speculative Serial

Treat worldbuilding as pressure on a character's choices, not as an encyclopedia. Establish what the protagonist wants before expanding the system, and reveal each rule through use, resistance, discovery, or cost.

## Workflow

1. Read the adopted premise, protagonist desire, governing speculative rule, price of power, opposition, and latest human revisions.
2. Read [strategy and quality](references/strategy-and-style.md) only for the current task.
3. Convert each new rule or discovery into a changed option, relationship, risk, or obligation.
4. Track knowledge boundaries, resources, capabilities, travel, time, setup/payoff, and irreversible costs.
5. Preserve project language, POV, tense, and character diction; explain only what the viewpoint needs now.
6. Review with textual evidence and the smallest repair that restores causality or wonder.

## Boundaries

- Combine with one platform Skill and the shared quality Skill.
- Do not use unexplained power escalation to resolve established conflict.
- Do not replace character consequence with lore delivery, generic spectacle, or a copied franchise pattern.
- Do not imitate living authors or reproduce distinctive terminology from reference works.
