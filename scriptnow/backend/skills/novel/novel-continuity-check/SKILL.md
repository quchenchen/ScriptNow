# Continuity audit before drafting

Perform these checks BEFORE writing a single word of the new chapter. Do not skip any step.

## 1. Character Inventory
- List every named character who appears in prior chapters.
- For each, note: full name, faction/pack/role, relationship to protagonist, current status (alive/dead/present/absent).
- Write NONE of these characters into the new chapter in a way that contradicts their established state.

## 2. Faction & Allegiance Map
- Every character belongs to exactly ONE faction/pack unless explicitly shown to switch.
- Cross-check all faction colors, titles, and territory names against prior chapters.
- If a character is seen wearing or using faction identifiers, they MUST match their established faction.

## 3. Name Audit
- Scan ALL proper names (people, places, organizations) for accidental duplicates.
- Two different characters MUST NOT share a surname unless a blood relation is explicitly established in text.
- If a surname repeats, either: (a) confirm the blood relation with a specific textual reference, or (b) change one character's surname.

## 4. Timeline & Causality
- Verify that the new chapter's events DO NOT contradict prior established facts.
- A character who died CANNOT appear alive without explanation.
- A document destroyed CANNOT be referenced as if still intact.
- An event described as happening "yesterday" CANNOT be described as "last week" in a later chapter.

## 5. Creative Graph Cross-Reference
- Compare proposed character actions against the creative graph's node descriptions.
- If a character is described as having a specific trait/goal, the new chapter must respect that trait or show a deliberate, motivated change.
- Do NOT introduce new characters who duplicate the role of an existing graph node without a clear narrative reason.

## Output
After completing these checks, state: "Continuity audit passed" before beginning the draft. If any violation is found, fix the plan before drafting.
