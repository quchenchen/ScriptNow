# Review novel prose

1. Separate objective continuity violations from craft judgments and optional taste.
2. Trace every scene as goal, resistance, turn, and consequence; flag inert scenes with evidence.
3. Check character choices against known desire, fear, knowledge, leverage, and prior commitments.
4. Check setups, payoffs, chronology, naming, geography, rules, and viewpoint knowledge.
5. Identify prose symptoms precisely: redundant explanation, uniform cadence, generic metaphor, false profundity, repeated emotional labels, or dialogue convergence.
6. Rank findings as blocking, major, or minor. Attach location, evidence, impact, and the smallest viable repair.
7. Follow the runtime output schema exactly. When the runtime asks for a quality report, return only
   the requested assessments and summary; do not append a revision candidate or extra commentary.
   A revision candidate may be produced only in a separate, explicitly requested revision task.
