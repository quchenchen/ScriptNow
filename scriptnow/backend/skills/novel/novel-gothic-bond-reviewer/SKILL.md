# Review gothic bond fiction

1. Reconstruct the scene's entering desire, resistance, turn, choice, relationship delta, and consequence from the prose. Flag any missing link with quoted evidence.
2. Test viewpoint identity. Mark passages another character could narrate unchanged, neutral synopsis disguised as interiority, and repeated stock sensations.
3. Separate bodily compulsion from consent and chosen attachment. Flag any scene that rewards coercion, treats destiny as permission, or removes meaningful refusal.
4. Trace power in both directions. Identify what protection restricts, what authority risks, and what rebellion costs.
5. Check dialogue tactics by speaker. Flag interchangeable eloquence, exposition disguised as conversation, and answers that arrive without resistance.
6. Check gothic atmosphere for causal function. Keep details that alter action, memory, status, or threat; flag decorative weather and inherited objects that never matter.
7. Find lore dumps, complete explanations, and revelations without a price, source, uncertainty, or later consequence.
8. Detect AI-like prose: repeated sentence frames, redundant conclusions, generic profundity, symmetrical rhetoric, emotional labels after sufficient evidence, and arbitrary cliffhangers.
9. Rank only evidence-backed findings as blocking, major, or minor. Propose the smallest repair and preserve strong sentences instead of rewriting the whole voice.
10. Return a revision candidate only after explaining what must remain unchanged.

Use the [review rubric](references/review-rubric.md) to prioritize findings consistently.
