# Review rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 2. Use scores to prioritize findings, not as a user-facing quality grade.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Viewpoint voice | interchangeable | occasional fingerprint | unmistakable selection and syntax |
| Relationship movement | declared only | one-sided or reversible | costly choice changes the relationship |
| Agency and consent | destiny replaces choice | choice exists but is weak | compulsion and choice remain distinct |
| Power consequence | decorative hierarchy | stated cost | power changes options and vulnerability |
| Atmosphere | decorative | supports mood | changes action and interpretation |
| Lore delivery | complete explanation | partial friction | evidence, conflict, uncertainty, consequence |
| Prose texture | repetitive or generic | mixed | varied, precise, character-bound |

Any zero in agency and consent is blocking. Any zero in viewpoint voice or relationship movement is major.
