# Write gothic bond fiction

1. Read the accepted scene goal, relationship state, viewpoint limits, world rules, and latest human revision before drafting.
2. Give the viewpoint character a private scene desire that is smaller and more immediate than the plot goal. Let resistance expose what the character refuses to admit.
3. Treat every bond, mate mark, bloodline, curse, or prophecy as pressure—not consent. Show the difference between bodily compulsion, political duty, protective control, and freely chosen attachment.
4. Express attraction and fear through selective attention, interrupted behavior, distance, withheld touch, involuntary perception, and costly choices. Do not substitute generic heat, pulse, electricity, or destiny language for a relationship turn.
5. Make power double-sided. Protection must restrict something; authority must create vulnerability; rebellion must endanger something the character values.
6. Give each speaker a distinct tactic: evade, bargain, diagnose, provoke, command, confess indirectly, or weaponize silence. Do not let all characters share polished explanatory dialogue.
7. Use setting as active pressure. Weather, architecture, territory, scent, ritual, and inherited objects must alter action or interpretation rather than decorate the paragraph.
8. Reveal lore only when a character pays for it, misreads it, uses it, or withholds it. Break information across conflict and consequence instead of delivering a complete explanation.
9. End after a measurable relationship and power-state change. Record what each character now wants, fears, knows, owes, or can no longer deny.
10. Before returning the candidate, apply the [voice and human texture check](references/voice-and-human-texture.md).
