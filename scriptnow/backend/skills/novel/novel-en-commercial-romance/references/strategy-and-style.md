# Commercial romance strategy

Define each lead’s external goal, attachment defense, moral line, and private need. Convert proximity, status, danger, contract, betrayal, or a second-life premise into choices. Make rupture causally earned and repair behaviorally demonstrated. Check consent and exit power; reject interchangeable rivals and trauma cured by romance.

For betrayal/regret stories, distinguish five beats: the original violation, the protagonist’s changed boundary, the betrayer’s loss of assumed access, evidence that changes interpretation without erasing harm, and a final choice with durable cost. “Chasing the wife” is not repair unless behavior, power, and future terms change.

For rebirth stories, define the remembered failure, what knowledge transfers, what cannot be known, and the first choice that breaks the old causal chain. Do not replay the previous life as exposition; reveal memory when it changes present action.

For family or rival conflict, give every escalation a new consequence. Avoid stacking interchangeable insults solely to delay the reversal.
