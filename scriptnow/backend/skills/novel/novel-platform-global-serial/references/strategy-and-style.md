# Global serial strategy and quality boundaries

## Story strategy

- State the reader promise in concrete dramatic terms, not only a trope label.
- Open on a consequential emotional collision. Establish only the minimum context needed to understand who is hurt, who has leverage, and what can be lost.
- Use information asymmetry deliberately: record who knows each fact, when the reader learns it, and how that difference changes a choice. Do not manufacture misunderstanding by withholding what a viewpoint character would naturally think.
- Escalate pressure through distinct state changes rather than repeated humiliation. Each reversal must alter evidence, leverage, allegiance, danger, intimacy, or public standing.
- Compress travel, routine, and connective explanation when they do not change the emotional or causal state; spend words on choice, reaction, and consequence.
- Build episodes around goal, resistance, choice, consequence, and a forward-bearing remainder.
- Re-anchor names, places, and stakes naturally after gaps without recapping the chapter.
- Let relationship turns and revelations emerge from action under pressure.

## Style reference

Prefer accessible, idiomatic English with varied sentence rhythm, specific sensory detail, and distinct character diction. A project may intentionally choose direct, Chinese-influenced commercial English, but grammar, pronoun reference, social behavior, and cultural setting must remain internally coherent. Mobile readability permits short paragraphs; it does not require simplistic thought, repetitive internal monologue, or generic cinematic description.

For short-form emotional serials, prefer a compact sequence:

1. collision or betrayal;
2. protagonist response that reveals a boundary or wound;
3. new evidence or information gap;
4. pressure escalation with a changed power balance;
5. choice, reversal, and a paid emotional consequence.

Treat this as a diagnostic spine, not a fixed plot template.

## Quality gate

- A reader can identify the protagonist, immediate pressure, genre promise, and unresolved question.
- Each episode changes knowledge, leverage, allegiance, danger, intimacy, or commitment.
- Pain escalates through causality; the text does not merely repeat increasingly cruel variants of the same beat.
- A “face-slap” or regret payoff follows observable choices and consequences instead of authorial punishment.
- Localisation is checked at the level requested by the project: names, institutions, family expectations, law, money, communication habits, and idiom. Preserve deliberate source flavour; remove accidental machine-translation artifacts.
- Voice remains stable across chapters and does not imitate a living author.
- Commercial tropes are transformed by character history and consequence.
- Platform rules and category labels are verified again at publication time.
