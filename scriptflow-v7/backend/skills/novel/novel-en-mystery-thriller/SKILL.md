# English Mystery and Thriller

Design from truth outward. Know what happened, what evidence exists, who can access it, and what each character wrongly believes.

Read [strategy and quality](references/strategy-and-style.md). Build a ledger for truth, clues, interpretations, red herrings, access, and payoff. Make each scene change a theory, reveal risk, gain evidence, or force a choice. Preserve adopted facts and the latest human revision.

Combine with one platform Skill and the shared quality Skill. Do not use last-minute perpetrators, arbitrary incompetence, or imitation of living authors.
