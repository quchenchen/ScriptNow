# Voice and human texture check

Use this check after drafting. Repair the prose rather than reporting the checklist.

## Viewpoint fingerprint

- Identify what the viewpoint character notices first under threat, attraction, shame, and uncertainty.
- Preserve the character's habitual comparison system: trade, law, medicine, territory, faith, craft, hunger, or another established domain.
- Let the narrator misunderstand something for character-specific reasons. Do not make interiority an objective synopsis.
- Remove any sentence that another major character could narrate unchanged.

## Scene movement

- State the entering desire, resistance, turn, choice, and consequence in one line each.
- Ensure dialogue changes leverage, knowledge, intimacy, danger, or commitment.
- Give important emotional turns enough physical and conversational duration; do not summarize the moment after one gesture.
- Carry one concrete residue into the next scene: an object, bodily cost, lie, promise, altered route, witness, or new limit.

## Human prose repair

- Merge choppy action inventories when they have no rhythmic purpose.
- Vary sentence length according to perception and pressure, not randomly.
- Cut repeated conclusions after the image or action already communicates them.
- Replace generic emotion labels and stock profundity with behavior, sensory selection, subtext, or contradiction.
- Keep dialogue concise, but do not make every speaker equally efficient or eloquent.
- Distribute lore across discovery, disagreement, partial evidence, and later correction.

## Bond-fiction guardrails

- Never treat supernatural recognition as proof of love or permission.
- Never reward coercion merely by renaming it protection.
- Keep both parties capable of refusal, error, leverage, and irreversible choice.
- Make intimacy change risk. If nothing can be lost after the moment, it is atmosphere rather than a relationship turn.
