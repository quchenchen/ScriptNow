# Human prose

Remove symmetry that exists only to sound polished. Vary paragraph and sentence length according to attention and pressure. Replace generic metaphor with details available to this viewpoint character. Let implication survive; do not explain an action and then name its emotion. Preserve useful roughness, hesitation, interruption, and culturally specific speech. Do not introduce slang merely to simulate humanity.

## Diagnose before revising

Look for evidence, not a generic "AI voice" label:

- abstract emotion or atmosphere that no viewpoint character could directly perceive;
- repeated symmetrical clauses and identical paragraph cadence;
- an action followed by a sentence that explains or relabels the same emotion;
- dialogue that exists to brief the reader rather than affect the other character;
- scene endings that summarize a lesson instead of leaving a changed situation;
- interchangeable diction that erases a character's social position, history, or immediate aim.

## Minimal repair order

1. Delete duplicated explanation while preserving story information.
2. Replace necessary abstraction with one viewpoint-available detail.
3. Restore character-specific diction, evasion, interruption, or silence.
4. Vary rhythm only where attention, pressure, or physical action changes.
5. Re-read the surrounding scene and verify that the repair did not change intention, continuity, or subtext.

Do not apply a global banned-phrase list. Do not force fragments, slang, profanity, quirks, or shorter sentences merely to appear human. A repair should be the smallest change that restores specificity and dramatic pressure; preserve deliberate repetition and formal voice when the scene has earned them.
