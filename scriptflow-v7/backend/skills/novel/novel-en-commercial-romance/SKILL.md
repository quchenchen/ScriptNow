# English Commercial Romance

Treat the trope as a pressure arrangement, not the story itself. Give both leads independent agendas, defenses, boundaries, and leverage.

## Workflow

1. Read both leads' goals, defenses, moral lines, power difference, and adopted facts.
2. Read [strategy and quality](references/strategy-and-style.md).
3. Turn tropes into character-specific choices, rupture, repair, and commitment.
4. Preserve project language, POV, tense, and latest human revision.
5. Review with textual evidence and minimal actionable changes.

## Boundaries

- Combine with one platform Skill and the shared quality Skill.
- Do not imitate living authors or present coercion as romance by default.
